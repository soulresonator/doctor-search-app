# Themes

## Design Goal

Create a healthcare application that feels:

* Professional
* Trustworthy
* Clean
* Modern
* Accessible

The UI should prioritize usability and clarity over visual effects.

---

## Design Style

* Modern Medical
* Minimalist
* Content-focused
* Mobile-first
* Responsive

AI may make reasonable design decisions as long as they align with the overall healthcare and professional tone.

---

## Theme

* Default: Light Mode
* Support: Dark Mode

---

## Colors

### Primary

Blue-based palette for trust and healthcare branding.

### Secondary

Teal or green accents for positive actions and healthcare-related highlights.

### Neutral

Slate/Gray palette for text, borders, and surfaces.

AI may choose specific shades while maintaining sufficient contrast and accessibility.

---

## Typography

* Font: Inter (preferred)
* Clean and readable hierarchy
* Accessibility first

---

## Components

Preferred style:

* Rounded corners
* Subtle shadows
* Clear visual hierarchy
* Consistent spacing

Avoid excessive decoration.

---

## Layout

* Mobile-first
* Responsive
* Simple navigation
* Prominent search functionality
* Easy access to doctor information and booking actions

---

## Accessibility

* WCAG-friendly contrast
* Keyboard accessibility (Web)
* Touch-friendly controls (Mobile)

---

## Avoid

* Glassmorphism
* Neumorphism
* Excessive animations
* Overly colorful interfaces
* Complex or distracting layouts

---

## Guiding Principle

When multiple design options are possible, prefer the solution that improves trust, readability, and ease of use for healthcare users.
